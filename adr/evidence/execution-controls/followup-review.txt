## Durable budgets
- Each reservation stores its timeout, ceiling, retry policy and `resumed_usage_stop` before process start (`:641-656`).
- When a record is loaded, each launch is checked against the ceiling it was reserved under (`:327-328`). Any continuation must have either the named usage-stop approval for the immediately previous launch, or that launch's status in its own recorded `retry.on` (`:330-344`).
- Lowering the ceiling stops further work but doesn't invalidate past launches, and raising it gives no refund: `test_extend_ceiling_and_policy_without_refunding`.
- `test_third_launch_cannot_use_ceiling_two` checks that a third launch can't be recorded under a ceiling of two.
- A failed reservation write marks the run as unusable (`:693-697`), and on reopen nothing has been spent. `test_approval_reservation_write_failure_does_not_consume_permission` confirms the record is byte-identical, the worker started once, there are no unresolved launches, and the later approved continuation delivers.

## Unresolved-launch recovery
- The stale-launch check runs before approval validation and again before reserving (`:605`, `:652`).
- `test_crash_after_approval_reservation_requires_recovery` shows an approved reservation that was never resolved blocks with `UncertainLaunchError` until `acknowledge_stopped` is called. The allowance is not refunded: the history ends up with 3 launches.

## Stop-specific, replay-safe approval
- An approval only takes effect when the named stop is the latest launch (`:626-630`), and only while allowance remains (`:631`).
- Repeating an approval after continuation or delivery just replays the result. A new usage stop needs a new approval. Later failures follow the ordinary retry policy.
- `RETRYABLE_STATUSES` (`models.py:25`) excludes `usage_limit` and `protocol_error`, so neither can be retried through the retry policy.
- Covered by `test_usage_approval_is_stop_specific_and_replay_safe`, `test_usage_approval_needs_remaining_allowance` and `test_approved_continuation_uses_ordinary_retries`.
